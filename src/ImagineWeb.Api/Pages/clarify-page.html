How to write a great prompt
1. WHAT — List key sections
Name the pages and features your site needs.
✗ "Make a website"
✓ "A portfolio site with Home, Projects, About, and Contact pages"
2. FORMAT — Describe the result
Specify layout style, framework, or tech preferences.
✗ "Make it nice"
✓ "Single-page app, dark theme, card-based layout"
3. CONTEXT — Who visits your site?
Help the AI understand your audience and purpose.
✗ "A business site"
✓ "A SaaS landing page targeting small business owners"
4. VISUALS — Describe the look
Mention colors, mood, images, animations.
✗ "Good design"
✓ "Minimalist, white + teal accent, large hero image, subtle scroll animations"
5. EXAMPLES — Point to references
Share links or names of sites you like for inspiration.
✗ "Something modern"
✓ "Similar to stripe.com — clean layout with gradient hero"
6. LIMITS — What to avoid
Explicitly exclude things you don't want.
✗ (nothing)
✓ "No stock photos, no carousels, no pop-ups"
You don't need all 6 — even 2-3 points dramatically improve results.
